IndexHot vs Manual Tracking: Same Method, Different Discipline

By IndexHot Team · November 16, 2026

Manual tracking is not the poor relation of tooling; it is the same method with fewer conveniences. A fixed prompt list, a spreadsheet and a written rubric will produce a usable baseline for a single brand. What manual tracking lacks is discipline under repetition, and that is what a published standard supplies.

When manual tracking is enough

One brand, four surfaces, fifty prompts, monthly. That is roughly three hours of work per cycle once the library is frozen, and it produces a baseline that is more defensible than most dashboards because you can point at every cell.

It is also the right first step commercially. Running a manual cycle tells you whether the gap is real before you commit to a subscription, and it gives you the prompt library that any tool will later need anyway.

Where manual tracking breaks

It breaks on repetition, not on rigour. The third month is when the coding standard starts to relax, when a level-2 claim gets scored as level-1 because the analyst is tired, and when nobody remembers why prompt 27 was reworded.

The fix is not a subscription; it is a written rubric, a calibration set and a version number. That is exactly what a published standard provides, and it is the only part of measurement that tooling cannot supply for you.

How to decide in ten minutes

Count the brands and surfaces you actually need. Up to three brands, manual. Beyond that, the time cost stops being trivial and tooling starts paying for itself.

Then ask whether you have written down what a citation means for your team. If the answer is no, you are not doing manual tracking yet, you are doing manual impressions, and the numbers will not survive a second reader.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is manual tracking accurate enough for reporting?

Yes for internal decisions and for trend, provided the prompt library is frozen and the rubric is written. It is less suited to weekly public claims, where cadence and consistency matter more than depth.

How long does a manual cycle take?

About three hours for one brand across four surfaces and fifty prompts, once the library exists. The first cycle takes longer because the library is being built.

What is the minimum viable setup?

A frozen list of fifty prompts, four platform columns, three outcome columns (mention, citation, absorption level), a date column, and one page describing what each absorption level means.
