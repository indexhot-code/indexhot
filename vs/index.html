What the distinction actually is

Most AI visibility tools answer one question: how often does a brand appear, and where. IndexHot answers a narrower one: can the number be reproduced by somebody else. That difference decides which tool fits, and it is why several of these comparisons end by recommending both instruments in sequence rather than either one alone.

  • If you need coverage across many brands and a trend line, a monitoring platform will do more for you than we can.
  • If you need to defend a number to someone who will check it, a published method is the cheaper route.
  • If you do not yet have a baseline, run fifty prompts by hand before buying anything.
