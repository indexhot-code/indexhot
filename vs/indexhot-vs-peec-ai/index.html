IndexHot vs Peec AI: Citation Analysis Compared

By IndexHot Team · November 16, 2026

Both approaches treat citations as the interesting part of AI visibility, which is where they agree. Peec AI builds citation analytics into a product with a clear interface; IndexHot publishes the rubric that decides what a citation is worth, so the judgement can be checked. Choose by whether you need analytics or auditability.

Where Peec AI is the better choice

When the operational question is which sources are winning in a category and how that moves week to week, a citation analytics product answers it far faster than a spreadsheet. Source breakdowns across many prompts are exactly what a dashboard is for.

It is also easier to hand to a non-specialist. A product interface with charts communicates faster than a rubric and a coded sample, and in most organisations the person reading the report is not the person who ran it.

Where IndexHot is different

A citation count answers how often your domain was attached. It does not answer whether the answer repeated anything you said. Our four-level rubric exists to separate those two, because a source-heavy engine can cite generously while compressing every source into near-anonymity.

The rubric is also the part that is easiest to get wrong silently. Two analysts scoring the same answer can differ by a level unless the levels are written down and calibrated. We publish the levels and the calibration procedure for that reason.

How to decide in ten minutes

If your reporting stops at citation counts and shares, analytics tooling is sufficient and faster. If a recommendation depends on the claim that your framing entered the answer, you need a rubric that defines what that means.

A practical test: take three answers where you were cited and ask whether you can say, without reading the pages, how much of your content survived. If you cannot, you are measuring attachment rather than influence.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as absorption at level 2?

A specific checkable claim from your page appearing in the answer in substance — a figure, a threshold, a defined condition — even if paraphrased. Level 1 is a theme you are associated with, which any competitor could also supply.

Can citation analytics products be scored with your rubric?

Yes. The rubric is platform-agnostic: it reads the answer text, not a vendor's score. Export the answers and code them. That is how we compare results across tools.

Which matters more, citation count or absorption?

For persuasion, absorption. For verification and discovery, citation count. They point to different fixes, which is why reporting one blended figure hides the thing you would act on.
