IndexHot vs Profound: Enterprise Depth or Public Method

By IndexHot Team · November 16, 2026

Profound is aimed at enterprise teams that need answer-level analysis and content guidance inside one platform. IndexHot is aimed at teams that need a method they can hand to someone else and have it verified. The split is between depth of tooling and reproducibility of measurement.

Where Profound is the better choice

At enterprise scale the constraint is coordination: several markets, several product lines, one owner of the AI visibility story. Profound is built for that, with analysis at the answer level and recommendations that connect findings to content work without a separate translation step.

If you need SSO, role-based access, and a vendor that will sign an enterprise agreement and support a quarterly business review, a product built for that procurement shape is the practical answer. A public method is not a substitute for those requirements.

Where IndexHot is different

Everything we publish can be checked. The prompt construction rules, the definition of a citation, the four-level absorption rubric and the scoring arithmetic are on one page, and the coded sample behind a reported number is exportable.

That is a narrow advantage, and a real one, for a specific audience: anyone whose output will be scrutinised. A research team citing a dataset, a consultancy defending a recommendation, a publisher whose competitors will attack the number in public.

How to decide in ten minutes

Ask whether your AI visibility number will ever be challenged by someone outside your company. If the answer is no, tooling depth is the better investment. If the answer is yes, an undisclosed scoring layer becomes a liability the moment it is questioned.

Ask secondly whether you need to change content this quarter. Given a clear brief, both approaches can produce one; given a report that needs to survive review, the documented one travels further.

Neither answer is permanent. Most organisations start with a public method to establish a baseline and move to platform tooling once the cadence is a standing commitment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Profound more accurate than a public standard?

Accuracy is not the axis. Both can be internally consistent. The difference is whether an outsider can reproduce the number, which is a property of disclosure rather than of model quality.

Can a small team use Profound?

Small teams usually cannot justify the cost of an enterprise platform before they have a baseline to justify it with. A manual run of fifty prompts is the cheaper way to find out whether the problem is real.

Does IndexHot have an enterprise offering?

Not currently. The offering is a standard, open datasets and a free manual report for the first twenty brands. Anything beyond that is a different product.
