Industry guide · Manufacturing

AI search visibility for manufacturers

Industrial buyers now ask assistants to shortlist suppliers by capability, tolerance, certification and lead time. A manufacturer that publishes complete, machine-readable specifications is shortlisted; one whose catalogue lives in a PDF or a login-gated portal is not.

Specifications are the product

In B2B sourcing, the decision is made on parameters long before a sales conversation. That makes specification completeness a visibility issue, not just an engineering one. Models filter suppliers by the constraints in the prompt — material, process, tolerance, certification, capacity — and a missing field removes you from the set.

  • Parameter tables in HTML. Not scanned PDFs, not images.
  • Certifications named explicitly. ISO, CE, RoHS and similar, with scope.
  • Capability boundaries. Stating what you cannot do prevents mismatched enquiries and improves match quality.

Where manufacturers lose sourcing queries

  • Product data only inside downloadable catalogues.
  • No machine-readable minimum order quantity or lead time.
  • Factory capability described in marketing adjectives instead of parameters.
  • No English-language technical pages for international buyers.

Manufacturing action plan

  1. Publish one page per product family with a complete HTML spec table.
  2. State MOQ, lead time, capacity and tolerances as numbers.
  3. List certifications with issuing body and scope.
  4. Add Product and Organization markup with consistent naming.
  5. Build capability pages around buyer constraints, not around your equipment list.

Frequently asked questions

Can manufacturers appear in AI sourcing recommendations?

Yes, when specifications, certifications and commercial terms are available as plain, structured text that the model can filter against the buyer's constraints.

Do PDF catalogues get read by AI assistants?

Rarely, and unreliably. HTML specification tables are far more likely to be extracted accurately than scanned or designed PDFs.

Why does stating limitations help visibility?

It improves match quality. Clear capability boundaries help the model exclude you from mismatched queries and include you in the right ones.
