Platform guide · Gemini

How brands appear in Gemini answers

Gemini is the most infrastructure-dependent of the major AI engines: it leans on Google's index, entity graph and structured data far more than on live retrieval alone. If your site is technically clean and your entity is unambiguous, you start with an advantage that pure content volume cannot buy.

Why Gemini rewards technical hygiene

Because Gemini sits inside Google's ecosystem, the classic technical signals still apply — but their payoff shows up inside generated answers rather than in a ranked list. Indexability, canonicalisation and entity consistency decide whether you are eligible at all; content quality then decides whether you are chosen.

  • Indexable, canonical pages. Duplicate or parameterised URLs dilute the entity signal and make grounding unreliable.
  • Structured data. Organization, Product, FAQ and Article markup help the model attach facts to the right entity.
  • Knowledge-graph consistency. Your name, logo, address and social profiles should agree across your site, Wikipedia/Wikidata, LinkedIn and review platforms.
  • Google-visible authority. Pages that already rank well for the query are far likelier to be used as grounding material.

What this means in practice

Teams often treat GEO and SEO as separate workstreams and then wonder why Gemini ignores them. In practice, Gemini is where the two overlap most: a page that cannot rank is rarely a page that gets grounded.

The good news is leverage. Fixing canonical tags, adding Organization markup and consolidating near-duplicate pages typically moves Gemini visibility faster than writing another ten blog posts.

Common failure modes

  • Brand name written three different ways across the site and profiles.
  • Key facts locked inside images, PDFs or client-rendered components.
  • Dozens of near-identical location or product pages with no canonical.
  • No visible authorship or contact information on YMYL topics.

Gemini checklist

  1. Audit canonical tags on every template, not just the homepage.
  2. Publish Organization markup with the same legal name used everywhere else.
  3. Consolidate thin duplicates into one authoritative page per topic.
  4. Keep core facts in server-rendered text with dates attached.
  5. Align your Wikipedia/Wikidata, LinkedIn and review-site profiles with your site.

Frequently asked questions

Does Gemini use Google search results?

Gemini is built on Google's infrastructure and draws heavily on the Google index and knowledge graph, so standard indexability and structured-data hygiene directly affect whether you can be grounded.

Is structured data important for Gemini?

Yes. Organization, Product, FAQ and Article markup help attach facts to your entity unambiguously, which reduces the chance of being confused with a competitor.

How is Gemini different from AI Overviews?

Both sit in Google's ecosystem, but Gemini is a conversational assistant while AI Overviews is a summarised block inside the results page. Gemini tolerates longer grounding passages; AI Overviews compresses harder.
