Frequently Asked Questions

How are these different from the blog?

The blog covers single findings and arguments. Guides are long-form references that cover a whole subject, with anchors and a table of contents so they can be read selectively.

Can I cite these guides?

Yes. The measurement definitions come from the published standard, which carries a stable citation. Attribute specific figures to the page you took them from.

Do you update them?

Each page carries a publication date and is revised when a claim stops holding. Corrections happen in the page rather than in a changelog.
