GEO Tools and Budget: Direct Answers

7 questions · answered directly

Most of what a GEO tool sells is repetition and record-keeping, not insight. The measurement itself is portable: a fixed prompt library, four surfaces and a coding sheet. Budget accordingly, and ask every vendor for the four things that make a score reproducible.

How much should a GEO tool cost?

A single brand tracking four surfaces monthly is a few hundred dollars a month at most, and often less than that. Multi-brand or agency portfolios with monthly reporting, version history and white-label exports run into four figures. Anything above that is usually paying for dashboards rather than measurement, and a dashboard cannot be audited.

Are GEO tool scores reliable?

Most proprietary scores are not reproducible, which is the definition of unreliable rather than wrong. Ask for four things: the prompt library, the platforms and dates of each run, the definition of a citation, and the rubric used for absorption. A vendor that cannot show all four is selling a number, not a measurement.

Should I hire an agency or build in-house?

Decide by which constraint you actually have. If the problem is execution capacity — pages to restructure, third-party records to correct — an agency helps. If the problem is judgement about which claims matter, that knowledge usually sits inside your own team, and outsourcing it produces generic pages. The common failure is hiring an agency for measurement when the real gap was content.

Can I do GEO with free tools only?

Yes, up to roughly twenty prompts a month across four surfaces. A spreadsheet with columns for prompt, platform, mention, citation, absorption level and notes is sufficient, and it forces the coding discipline that paid dashboards often hide behind an aggregate score. Free browser access to each platform covers the data collection.

What should I ask a GEO vendor before buying?

Five questions. Which prompts, and who chose them? Which platforms, run on what dates? How is a citation defined, and how is absorption scored? Can I export the raw coded answers? What happens to my baseline when you change the methodology? The last one matters most, because silent methodology changes are how trend lines become fiction.

Is it worth paying for AI visibility monitoring?

It is worth paying for consistency, not for discovery. If nobody on the team will run fifty prompts on the same day each month, a monitoring subscription buys reliability you would otherwise lose. If the team will run it, the subscription mostly buys formatting.

How do I budget GEO against SEO?

Treat it as a reallocation rather than a new line item for the first two quarters. The crawl, content and internal-link work overlaps heavily, and the measurable new costs are measurement time and third-party correction. Once citations are tracked, a separate budget makes sense, typically ten to twenty percent of the combined search budget at maturity.
