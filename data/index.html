How to cite

If you use these datasets in research or reports, please cite them as:

Plain text

BibTeX

@misc{indexhot2026data,
  title={IndexHot GEO Data 2026},
  author={IndexHot},
  year={2026},
  url={https://indexhot.com/data/}
}
