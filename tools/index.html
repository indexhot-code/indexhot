Free GEO tools

Three tools · no account · runs in your browser

None of these tools sends your input anywhere. They exist because the three hardest parts of this work — knowing what a score means, knowing what to ask, and knowing whether a page can be quoted — are cheap to compute and expensive to get wrong. Use them before you buy anything.

The tools

GEO Score Calculator Turn mention, citation and absorption rates into an IndexHot Score and a band. Prompt Library Generator Build the fifty-prompt baseline, split ten branded and forty non-branded. Citation Extractability Checker Paste a page section and see whether a summary could lift a usable claim.

What they will not do

They do not query ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or AI Overviews, because no browser tool can do that on your behalf without an account and without violating those platforms' terms. What they do is the reasoning around the measurement: the arithmetic, the question set, and the structural check.

The actual running of prompts is manual, and it stays manual here on purpose. Fifty prompts across four surfaces takes about three hours, and doing it yourself is what makes the numbers yours rather than a vendor's.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes, with no account and no usage limit. They run locally in your browser, so there is no server cost to recover.

Do they work offline?

Yes. The whole site is static HTML with a local stylesheet. Load the page once and the tools keep working without a connection.

Is my pasted text stored anywhere?

No. The checker uses regular expressions and word counts in memory. Nothing is transmitted, logged or saved.
