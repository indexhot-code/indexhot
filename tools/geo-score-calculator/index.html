GEO Score Calculator

Enter your mention, citation and absorption rates to get an IndexHot Score and the band it falls into.

The IndexHot Score is 100 × (0.30 · MR + 0.30 · CR + 0.40 · AR), where the three rates are expressed as fractions of the prompts you ran. Because absorption carries the largest weight, a brand that is named often but never quoted will score lower than a brand that appears less often and is quoted more.

What the bands mean

The four bands describe what a score implies for a buyer, not how much work you have done.

Score Band What it implies
0–25 Invisible The model does not name you for these prompts. Usually an access or entity problem before it is a content problem.
26–55 Mentioned You are named, but rarely attached as a source. Your pages are not being treated as evidence.
56–80 Cited Sources are landing on pages you own. The next constraint is usually extractability rather than distribution.
81–100 Recommended Your framing is being repeated. This is the band where a mention turns into a shortlist position.

Combining three platforms

For a cross-platform figure, score each platform separately and average with default weights of ChatGPT 0.40, Perplexity 0.30 and Gemini 0.30. Do not average the raw rates across platforms first: a mention on one surface and a citation on another are different achievements that cancel out when blended.

Where a fourth surface matters commercially, add it as an explicit weight and record the change, because a silently reweighted composite is indistinguishable from a methodology change when someone reads last quarter's number.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does absorption carry 40 percent?

Because it is the only component that measures influence rather than presence. A brand cited a hundred times whose claims never appear in the answer has bought distribution without persuasion, and the weighting is designed to make that visible rather than flattering.

Can I use this score for my own tool?

Yes. The formula and band thresholds are published under the measurement standard and can be reproduced without attribution. What we ask is that you keep the three inputs separate in your reporting, because a single blended number is not auditable.

What if my rates come from different prompt sets?

Then the score is not comparable to anything, including your own previous score. All three rates must come from the same frozen library and the same run, which is the only condition this calculator assumes.
