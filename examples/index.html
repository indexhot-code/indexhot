Worked examples: how the method runs end to end

Two runs of the method on invented brands, published so the sequence can be checked rather than admired. Every figure is constructed; the reasoning is not.

The examples

A B2B SaaS visibility audit, start to second run Fifty prompts, both halves reported separately, and the failure located by cluster rather than by page. Rewriting one product section for extraction A passage that fails four tests, the rewrite that passes them, and what each change buys.

Why these are illustrative, not case studies

Most agencies publish case studies built on numbers nobody can check, and the honest ones publish almost nothing because clients do not want their weaknesses described in public. Both outcomes leave the reader without a method.

So the examples here use invented brands. That removes the incentive to flatter a result, and it lets the numbers be chosen to show the shape of a change rather than to prove one. What is real is the sequence, the coding rules and the reasoning about where the loss sits.

What a worked example is for

  • To show the order of operations, from prompt library to cluster-level diagnosis to a change that can be traced.
  • To show what a failing page looks like next to a working one, which is easier to copy than an abstract principle.
  • To make the standard checkable: if your own report has no unbranded column, these pages show what is missing.

Treat them as worked exercises. Run the same sequence on your own domain, with your own prompts, and expect your numbers to differ in every cell.

Frequently asked questions

Will you publish real case studies?

Only where a client agrees to be named and to have their starting numbers shown, including the bad ones. Until then, illustrative examples and the public data page carry the burden.

Can I cite these numbers?

Cite the method, not the figures. The figures are constructed and should never be quoted as a benchmark.

Why not use anonymised client data?

Because anonymisation is easy to reverse in small categories, and the point of these pages can be made without borrowing anyone's position.
