Frequently asked questions

What is AI search visibility? +

AI search visibility describes how often and how prominently your brand appears in answers generated by AI assistants and search engines, such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. It covers whether you are mentioned, cited as a source, and recommended ahead of competitors.

How does IndexHot measure brand mentions? +

We run a fixed, governed set of branded and non-branded questions on each platform, then code every answer for three things: whether your brand is named (mention), whether your domain is linked (citation), and whether your own claims or data are adopted (absorption). The results combine into a transparent, reproducible score.

Is IndexHot free to use? +

Yes. The first 20 brands receive a free manual report with no credit card required. Just enter your domain, email and brand name, and your report will arrive within 24 hours.

Which AI platforms does IndexHot track? +

We currently track ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, using consistent prompts and settings across all of them so the results are directly comparable.
