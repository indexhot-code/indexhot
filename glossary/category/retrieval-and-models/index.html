Retrieval and models

The mechanics between a question and an answer: how documents are found, split, ranked and turned into sentences.

Almost every puzzling result in an AI visibility report resolves to one of these terms. A page cited for a question it answers in one paragraph and ignored elsewhere is passage retrieval. A citation with nothing of yours in the answer is snippet selection or a grounding problem.

None of these mechanisms is directly controllable. What is controllable is the shape of the material they operate on, which is why the definitions matter more to editors than the engineering diagrams do.

Frequently asked questions

Is retrieval the same as search?

Search ranks documents; retrieval here also covers splitting them into passages and selecting the passages that answer a query.

Why do different surfaces behave differently?

Because they combine these mechanisms differently: some attach many sources, some few, some hedge and some assert.

Can I influence which passage is retrieved?

Indirectly, by making each section answer one question completely and opening with a self-contained claim.
