Measurement

The vocabulary for counting what an answer says. Fourteen of these terms predate this expansion; six were added to cover states that a single visibility number cannot express, such as drift, gaps between branded and non-branded prompts, and how much of an answer a brand occupies.

Measurement terms exist to prevent a specific error: reporting presence as influence. Mention, citation and absorption are three different facts, and the arithmetic that combines them is meaningless unless each is coded separately.

If you only take two terms from this group, take absorption rate and governed question library. The first is the only metric that measures influence; the second is what makes any of the others comparable over time.

Mention Rate the share of tracked prompts in which your brand is named Citation Rate the share of prompts in which your domain is linked as a source Absorption Rate the share of prompts where your specific claims enter the answer Citation Absorption how deeply a cited source's content is reused in the answer Share of Voice your brand's share of mentions within a tracked prompt set Citation attribution of a domain as a source inside an AI answer Branded Prompt a question that includes the brand name explicitly Non-Branded Prompt a category question asked without naming any brand Governed Question Library a fixed, versioned set of prompts used for every measurement run AI Visibility Index a ranked index of brands scored on the same AI prompt set Citation Gap the difference between being mentioned and being cited as a source Answer Sentiment the tone an AI answer uses when it discusses your brand Long-Tail Prompt a specific, constrained question with clear intent and low volume Citation Source Diversity how spread out the sources behind an AI answer are Answer Drift The tendency of an identical prompt to return a materially different answer over time, without any change on your side. Visibility Gap The difference between how visible a brand is on branded prompts and how visible it is on non-branded ones. Prompt Cluster A group of prompts that share one buyer intent, scored together because they fail for the same reason. Share of Answer How much of an answer's text and structure is occupied by one brand relative to the others named. Brand Recall Whether a model names a brand without being prompted by its name, from memory rather than retrieval. Source Concentration How few domains account for most citations in a category's answers.

Frequently asked questions

Which metric should a report lead with?

Absorption rate. It is the one that predicts whether the answer persuaded anyone, and it is the one most often missing.

Is share of answer part of the standard?

Not in v1.0. It is defined here because it is widely used informally and needs a shared meaning.
