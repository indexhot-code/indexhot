Measurement

The largest group, because measurement is where most of the ambiguity lives: which rate, over what sample, against which baseline.

The vocabulary here exists to keep four things separate that are routinely conflated: was the brand named, was a source of yours attached, was anything of yours reused, and how much of the sampled set did any of that happen in.

Two further distinctions run through the group. Branded and non-branded prompts are reported separately because they measure recall and discovery respectively. And every rate needs its denominator stated, since a percentage without a sample size is the most common defect in this field.

Frequently asked questions

Which single number should a report lead with?

None. Report the three rates separately per surface, with the branded and unbranded halves split, and let the reader see the shape.

What sample size is enough?

Enough that one answer changing moves the rate by only a point or two; in practice tens of prompts per group, not five.

Why separate absorption from citation?

Because you can be cited and not repeated, and that pattern needs a different fix from being absent.
