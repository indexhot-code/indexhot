Entity and content

What a model believes about who you are, and the structures that make that belief consistent. This group covers clarity, schema, authorship and the trust framework that governs whether a claim is usable.

Entity work is upstream of content work. A page that answers a question perfectly is wasted if the model cannot tell which company the answer is about, or if it attributes the claim to a similarly named competitor.

The practical order is: state the entity plainly, declare it in schema, corroborate it elsewhere, then publish. Skipping the first three produces pages that are accurate about the wrong subject.

Frequently asked questions

Does schema fix entity confusion?

It corroborates, but contradictory prose elsewhere can outweigh it. Consistency across sources matters more than the presence of markup.

What is the cheapest entity fix?

One sentence stating category, location and product, repeated in the same form on the homepage, the about page and every external profile.
