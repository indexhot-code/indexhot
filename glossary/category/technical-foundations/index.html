Technical foundations

The conditions under which content can be fetched, indexed and cited at all. When these fail, no content work reaches the answer, and the symptom looks identical to being outranked.

These terms describe plumbing, and plumbing failures are silent. A page can look correct in a browser, pass a human review and still be unreadable to a fetcher that does not execute scripts or cannot reach it through internal links.

Check them first when a whole cluster is absent, not when a single passage fails. Absence across many prompts is almost always a technical condition rather than a writing problem.

Content Extractability how easily a machine can isolate, quote and reuse your content Crawlability whether crawlers can discover and fetch your pages Indexability whether a page is eligible to be stored in a search index Canonical Tag a signal declaring the preferred URL for a piece of content Internal Linking links between your own pages that distribute authority and signal structure AI Referral Traffic visits that arrive from an AI answer or assistant Competitive Citation Share your share of cited sources versus competitors in a prompt set AI Crawler a bot that fetches content for model training or live retrieval Content Freshness how current a page is, and how clearly it says so Crawl Budget The amount of crawling a site can expect, and how efficiently that allowance is spent. Render Blocking Content that only appears after JavaScript runs, and is therefore invisible to some crawlers and fetchers. Status Code Hygiene Whether the site returns the correct HTTP status for every kind of URL: 200, 301, 404 or 410. Content Decay The gradual loss of citation and traffic as a page's facts age or as better-structured competitors appear. Crawl Log The record of which URLs a crawler requested and what each returned.

Frequently asked questions

Which technical failure is most common?

Content that only exists after JavaScript runs. It is invisible in browser-based audits and decisive for retrieval pipelines.

How do I test it in five minutes?

Fetch the URL as raw HTML and read what appears. If the substance is missing, nothing downstream will see it.
