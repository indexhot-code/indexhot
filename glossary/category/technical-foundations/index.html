Technical foundations

The conditions that decide whether content is reachable at all: access rules, rendering, indexing, status codes and crawl diagnostics.

Every term here is a gate. Failing one produces the same symptom as bad content, absence, which is why technical failures get diagnosed as editorial ones and rewritten for months.

The checks are cheap and finishable, which is the argument for doing them first: they clear the denominator that all later content work is measured against.

Frequently asked questions

Do these matter more than content?

No, but they come first, because content cannot be retrieved from a page that was never fetched.

How often should they be checked?

Quarterly, and immediately after any front-end, firewall or hosting change.

Is this the same as classic technical SEO?

Largely, with two additions: separate crawler agents for retrieval, and render parity under a latency budget.
