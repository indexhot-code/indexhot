What is Content Freshness?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

Content freshness is the degree to which a page reflects current facts, combined with how explicitly it signals its recency through visible dates and machine-readable timestamps.

In short: How current a page is, and how clearly it says so.

Why it matters

Answer systems prefer sources they can date. Stale figures are a leading cause of wrong answers about pricing, availability and policy, and a model that cannot tell when a page was written has to hedge or substitute another source.

How to work with it

Show a visible last-updated date, keep dateModified in structured data in sync, and remove or clearly archive superseded figures rather than leaving them to be retrieved.

Common mistakes

  • Updating a page without changing its dateModified value.
  • Leaving outdated prices or statistics in body copy after a revision.
  • Dating content in prose only, with no machine-readable timestamp.

Real example

A pricing page showed 2023 tiers with no updated date. Assistants quoted the old prices for eleven months after a 2024 change, and two prospects cited them in lost-deal notes. Adding a visible date and syncing dateModified corrected most answers.

How to measure it

Compare the visible last-updated date, the dateModified value and the newest figure on the page. Any mismatch tells systems the page cannot be trusted on time-sensitive facts.

Where it breaks

Freshness helps factual queries and does little for conceptual ones. Republishing an evergreen explainer with a new date and no new content teaches systems that dates are decorative.

How it differs from adjacent terms

Crawlability gets a page fetched; freshness decides whether it is trusted on time-sensitive facts. Compared with Hallucination, freshness is prevention: stale figures are a leading cause of confidently wrong answers, and most of those errors trace to a page nobody updated rather than to invention.

Quick checklist

  • Visible last-updated date on every factual page.
  • dateModified in markup matching the real revision.
  • Superseded figures removed, not left beside new ones.
  • Prices, policies and availability reviewed on a schedule.
  • Dates never updated without a content change.

FAQ

Does freshness affect AI citations?

It affects source selection when an answer depends on current facts. For evergreen conceptual content, structure and clarity matter more than recency.

How often should I update a page?

When the facts change, not on a calendar. A page updated with a new date but identical content teaches systems that the timestamp is unreliable.
