What is Brand Entity?

A brand entity is the representation of your brand as a distinct object in a machine's knowledge model, defined by attributes such as name, domain, products, founders, locations and relationships to other entities.

In short: Your brand as a machine-resolvable object with attributes and relations.

Why it matters

Entities are the anchor points answers are built on. If the entity is thin or contradictory, a model substitutes a generic description ('a project management tool') for your differentiator, and your specifics never enter the answer.

How to work with it

Decide one canonical name and one canonical domain, state the relationship between brand and products explicitly, and corroborate the same facts across your site, profiles and directories.

Common mistakes

  • Letting the product name overshadow the brand name with no stated link.
  • Using a different name on every external profile.
  • Describing the category differently on each page.

FAQ

Is a brand entity the same as entity clarity?

Related but distinct. The entity is the object; clarity is how unambiguously a system can resolve it. You need a well-formed entity before clarity becomes measurable.

Do I need a Wikipedia page to have a brand entity?

No. Consistent structured data, corroborating profiles and unambiguous on-site descriptions can establish an entity without one.
