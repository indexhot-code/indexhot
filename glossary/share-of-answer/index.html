What is Share of Answer?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

Share of answer measures the proportion of an AI response devoted to a given brand, in words or in structural weight, when several brands are mentioned. It differs from share of voice, which counts prompts won, and from share of citations, which counts links.

In short: How much of an answer's text and structure is occupied by one brand relative to the others named.

Why it matters

Being one of six names in a list is different from being the two names the answer actually develops. Share of answer captures that difference, and it correlates more closely with influence than count-based metrics.

It also explains a common disappointment: a brand whose mention rate rose while nothing changed commercially, because the new mentions were single-word inclusions at the end of a list.

How to work with it

Code each answer for the brands named, then estimate the approximate proportion of the answer devoted to each.

  • Count developed mentions, where the answer makes a claim about the brand, separately from bare inclusions.
  • Note structural position: first name in a list, the subject of a paragraph or a parenthetical at the end.
  • Aggregate at the cluster level; a brand can hold large share in one cluster and none in another.

Common mistakes

  • Treating every mention as equal weight, which inflates list-tail inclusions.
  • Measuring only word count, which misses table rows and bullet items where a brand may dominate structurally.
  • Comparing share of answer across surfaces that format answers differently without noting the format.

FAQ

Is share of answer a standard metric?

Not yet. It is used in practice and defined here so it can be applied consistently; it is not part of the v1.0 composite.

How do I estimate it without heavy tooling?

Rough proportion is enough. The useful signal is whether a brand is developed or merely listed, not a decimal place.
