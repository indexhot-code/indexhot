What is Visibility Gap?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

The visibility gap is the delta between mention rate on prompts that name the brand and mention rate on prompts that describe the need without naming it. A large gap means the brand is recognised when prompted but not considered when the buyer is choosing.

In short: The difference between how visible a brand is on branded prompts and how visible it is on non-branded ones.

Why it matters

Branded prompts measure memory; non-branded prompts measure acquisition. A brand can score 90 percent on the first and 15 percent on the second, and the composite hides exactly the number that matters commercially.

The gap is also the most useful single diagnostic in GEO, because it separates two problems with different fixes: recognition is about the entity being clear, consideration is about pages answering the buyer's actual questions.

How to work with it

Split your library by prompt type and report the two rates separately, then compute the delta.

  • Branded rate: brand named in the prompt. Non-branded rate: need described, brand not named.
  • A gap under 15 points usually means the brand is genuinely considered. A gap over 40 points means almost all visibility is borrowed from the prompt itself.
  • Track the gap per intent cluster. A brand can be considered for comparisons and invisible for implementation questions.

Common mistakes

  • Reporting one blended visibility number that averages a 90 and a 15 into a reassuring 52.
  • Assuming a high branded rate means the market knows you; it only means the model does.
  • Ignoring clusters inside the gap, which makes the fix look like "write more content" instead of "answer these six questions".

FAQ

Why is the gap usually larger for smaller brands?

Because models have thinner evidence about them. When the brand is named, retrieval can find the site; when it is not named, there is nothing to retrieve it against.

Can a brand close the gap without new pages?

Sometimes. Pages that answer the cluster's actual questions, with the brand named inside the answer, often move non-branded rates without adding volume.
