What is Absorption Lift?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

Absorption lift is the absorption delta attributable to one change: a rewritten passage, a new boundary, a published table. It is measured on the affected prompts, with an untouched set as control, so that drift is not mistaken for improvement.

In short: The absorption change for one edited page or cluster, measured against a control.

Why a control is required

Answer surfaces drift between runs. If you measure absorption before and after an edit without a control, you are measuring the edit plus the drift, and the two are indistinguishable in the result. A control set of comparable prompts, untouched during the change, gives you the drift estimate for the same period.

Designing a small experiment

  1. Pick a cluster where the page is already cited and absorption is L0 or L1.
  2. Freeze twenty prompts: ten affected, ten comparable control prompts on similar pages.
  3. Run the full set twice before the edit, to establish the noise floor on those prompts.
  4. Make one change, not three. Rewrite the claim, or add the boundary, not both.
  5. Run twice after, at least one cycle later, since absorption moves last.

Reading the result honestly

Lift below the noise floor is not a result, even if the direction is right. Lift on affected prompts with no movement in the control is the shape you want; both moving together means the surface changed, not your page.

PatternInterpretation
Affected up, control flat
The edit worked

Why this is the only honest attribution

Programme-level absorption movement cannot be tied to a page. Cluster-level lift with a control can, and it is the smallest unit at which the claim this edit caused this survives review.

Frequently asked questions

How long until lift appears?

Usually one full measurement cycle, sometimes two. Absorption is the slowest of the three outcomes.

Can I run this without a control?

You can run it; you cannot interpret it. The control is what separates your edit from drift.

How many affected prompts?

Ten is workable; fewer than five makes a single answer change dominate the number.
