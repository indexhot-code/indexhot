What is Prompt Drift?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

Prompt drift is the gradual shift in the wording and intent of real user questions over time. A library frozen last year measures last year's questions, and the resulting flat line can be mistaken for stability.

In short: The slow change in what people actually ask an assistant, which quietly invalidates a fixed prompt library.

Two kinds of drift

Wording drift is vocabulary change: a category gets renamed, a competitor's brand becomes the generic term, a regulation introduces new language. Intent drift is structural: questions move from what is this to how do I switch, as a market matures.

Both reduce the value of a fixed set, and both are invisible in a report that only shows rates. A rate that holds steady while the underlying questions changed is a measurement of your library, not of your market.

Where to look for it

  • Search console queries for the same topics, compared year over year.
  • Sales call notes and support tickets, which contain the phrasing buyers actually use.
  • Community threads and review sites, where category language is negotiated.
  • The answers themselves: when every model starts prefacing with a definition, the question has become unfamiliar to a new audience.

Why not simply rewrite the library

Because comparability is the point. A library that changes whenever someone has a hunch produces numbers that cannot be compared across quarters, which is the same as having no numbers. Drift should trigger a scheduled revision, not an immediate edit.

The workable pattern is a versioned library revised on a fixed cadence, with the retiring prompts and the reasons recorded. Then a change in the rate can be attributed to the market or to the library, and usually to both, in known proportions.

How it shows up in a report

The tell is a set where several prompts produce nearly identical answers across all brands. Those prompts have been overtaken: the question is now settled, and continuing to ask it measures the settlement rather than your position.

Frequently asked questions

How often does drift matter?

Annually for stable B2B categories, quarterly where a market is consolidating or a regulation is landing.

Can I detect it without new data?

Partly. Re-read your own library and ask whether a stranger would still phrase a question that way. That catches wording drift cheaply.

Does drift invalidate old reports?

It dates them. Keep the version annotation and the old reports remain honest history.
