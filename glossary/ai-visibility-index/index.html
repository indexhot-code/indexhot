What is AI Visibility Index?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

An AI visibility index is a published ranking of brands scored on a fixed, shared prompt set under a documented method, so that relative visibility in AI answers can be compared across companies and over time.

In short: A ranked index of brands scored on the same AI prompt set.

Why it matters

Individual brand checks answer 'how am I doing'. An index answers 'where do I stand'. It also creates a citable artefact: journalists, analysts and AI systems themselves reference indices, which generates the third-party mentions that strengthen entity signals.

How to work with it

Publish the method, the prompt set summary, the scoring formula and the measurement date, then re-publish on a fixed schedule so the trend is legible.

Common mistakes

  • Building an index on a sample too small to be stable.
  • Publishing ranks without the method, which makes them unverifiable and unciteable.
  • Changing the prompt set between editions without re-baselining.

Real example

A quarterly index covering 40 brands in one category was cited by three trade publications and referenced in assistant answers about the category itself. The ranking became a source, which is a form of visibility the entrants could not buy.

How to measure it

Publish the method, sample size, scoring formula and measurement window before publishing the ranking. Without those, readers cannot compare editions and journalists will not cite it.

Where it breaks

Indices are easy to game and easy to discredit. Small samples and undisclosed methods invite both outcomes, and the reputational cost lands on the publisher.

How it differs from adjacent terms

Share of Voice is a metric you compute privately; an AI visibility index is a published ranking others can cite. Compared with AI Search Visibility, which describes one brand, an index creates a comparative market artefact. That publication is the point: rankings become third-party sources, which is a form of visibility no single brand can buy.

Quick checklist

  • Sample size stated and large enough to be stable.
  • Method, formula and measurement window published.
  • Prompt set summarised publicly, even if not listed in full.
  • Edition dates fixed in advance and kept.
  • Re-baselining done explicitly whenever the set changes.

FAQ

Is an AI visibility index the same as a benchmark report?

A report describes findings; an index produces a repeatable ranking. The index is more useful to readers who want to compare themselves against peers.

How often should an index be refreshed?

Quarterly is typical. More frequent editions add noise without enough change in the underlying answers to justify them.
