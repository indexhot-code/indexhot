What is Mention Rate (MR)?

Mention Rate (MR) is the share of prompts in a fixed question library where your brand name appears anywhere in the AI answer, regardless of whether your domain is cited or your claims are used.

In short: The share of tracked prompts in which your brand is named.

Why it matters

Mention is the entry-level signal: it tells you whether the model recognises the brand at all. It is the cheapest metric to move and the easiest to over-interpret, because a passing mention can carry no recommendation.

How to work with it

MR = prompts where the brand is mentioned / total prompts run. Always report it alongside Citation Rate and Absorption Rate.

Common mistakes

  • Celebrating MR growth while citation and absorption stay flat.
  • Counting mentions of a similarly named but different company.
  • Comparing MR across platforms without equal prompt counts.

FAQ

What is a good mention rate?

It depends on the category and the prompt mix. What matters is the direction of travel against a stable baseline and how it compares with competitors in the same answers.

Why does mention rate differ between branded and non-branded prompts?

Branded prompts almost guarantee a mention if the brand exists. Non-branded prompts reveal whether the model recommends you when the user has not named you, which is the harder and more valuable signal.

Related terms

Citation Rate the share of prompts in which your domain is linked as a source Absorption Rate the share of prompts where your specific claims enter the answer Branded Prompt a question that includes the brand name explicitly
