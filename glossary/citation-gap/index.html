What is Citation Gap?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

A citation gap is the difference between the prompts where your brand is mentioned and the prompts where your domain is actually cited as a source, or the equivalent difference between you and a competitor.

In short: The difference between being mentioned and being cited as a source.

Why it matters

The gap tells you what kind of problem you have. A large mention-without-citation gap points to extractability, crawlability or grounding issues rather than missing content. A gap against a competitor points to authority or coverage.

How to work with it

Compute it per platform, list the specific prompts in the gap, then inspect the pages those answers do cite to see what they offer that yours does not.

Common mistakes

  • Treating every gap as a content production problem.
  • Fixing gaps across all platforms at once instead of the largest one.
  • Measuring the gap once rather than tracking whether it closes after changes.

Real example

A brand was mentioned in 38 of 50 prompts and cited in 7. Inspecting the answers showed competitors were cited via comparison tables and specification pages. Publishing two comparison pages and a spec sheet closed the gap to 24 of 50 in six weeks.

How to measure it

List the specific prompts sitting in the gap, then read what those answers actually cite. The cited pages reveal the content shape your site is missing.

Where it breaks

Not every gap is closable. Where the cited sources are independent review sites, the fix is earned media and listings rather than on-site content.

How it differs from adjacent terms

Citation Rate tells you the level; the citation gap tells you the distance and its shape. Compared with Citation Source Diversity, the gap is about your own shortfall, while diversity describes the whole competitive field. Reading them together distinguishes a content problem from a distribution problem, which need different budgets.

Quick checklist

  • Gap computed per platform, not blended.
  • Each gap prompt traced to the pages actually cited.
  • Gaps split into mention-without-citation and competitor-citation.
  • Fix prioritised by prompt commercial value, not count.
  • Gap re-measured after each change rather than assumed closed.

FAQ

What is the fastest way to close a citation gap?

Usually by restructuring existing pages, not by writing new ones. Clear, self-contained, well-marked-up passages convert mentions into citations more reliably than additional volume.

Can a mention without a citation still be valuable?

Yes, if the answer carries your positioning. But it is fragile: without a source, the claim is unverifiable and easy for a competitor's framing to replace.
