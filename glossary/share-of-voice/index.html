What is Share of Voice (SOV)?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

Share of Voice (SOV) in AI search is your brand's proportion of all brand mentions across a tracked prompt set, relative to the competitors that also appear in those answers.

In short: Your brand's share of mentions within a tracked prompt set.

Why it matters

Absolute visibility can rise while relative position falls, because the category grows. SOV keeps the comparison honest and shows whether you are gaining the answer share that competitors are losing.

How to work with it

SOV = your mentions / all tracked brand mentions. Define the competitor set in advance and keep it stable between cycles.

Common mistakes

  • Using an arbitrary competitor list that changes every report.
  • Counting only direct rivals and missing substitutes named in answers.
  • Reporting SOV on a prompt set that includes branded queries for your own name.

Real example

A brand doubled its mentions year over year and still lost four points of share, because the category tripled and two new entrants entered the answer set. Absolute growth masked relative decline.

How to measure it

Fix the competitor list in advance, exclude your own branded prompts, and report share alongside absolute counts so growth and position are both visible.

Where it breaks

Share of voice says nothing about sentiment or depth. A brand can hold the largest share and still be described negatively in every answer.

How it differs from adjacent terms

Mention Rate measures you in isolation; share of voice measures you against everyone else in the same answers. Compared with Competitive Citation Share, share of voice counts mentions while citation share counts attributed sources, so a brand can lead one and trail the other. Both need the same fixed competitor list to be comparable over time.

Quick checklist

  • Competitor list documented and frozen between cycles.
  • Branded prompts excluded from the competitive calculation.
  • Absolute counts reported beside the share.
  • Category growth noted, since it dilutes share mechanically.
  • New entrants added only at a scheduled revision.

FAQ

Should SOV include branded prompts?

Usually not for competitive comparison, because branded prompts inflate your share by definition. Report branded and non-branded separately.

How many competitors should I track?

Three to six is typical. Enough to reflect the real choice set, few enough to keep coding reliable.
