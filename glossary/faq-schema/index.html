What is FAQ Schema?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

FAQ schema is structured data using the FAQPage type that declares question-and-answer pairs on a page, so machines can identify an explicit question and its corresponding answer.

In short: Markup that pairs questions with answers for machine extraction.

Why it matters

A question paired with a clean answer is the most extractable content shape that exists, which is why FAQ markup aligns so directly with answer engine optimisation. It also makes the page's coverage of a topic legible to retrieval systems.

How to work with it

Mark up real questions the page visibly answers, keep answers to two or three sentences, and mirror the visible wording exactly.

Common mistakes

  • Marking up FAQs that are collapsed or absent from the rendered page.
  • Writing marketing answers that do not actually answer the question.
  • Inventing questions nobody asks in order to inflate coverage.

Real example

A SaaS site marked up 22 invented FAQs per page to look comprehensive. Rich results stopped appearing and the pages read as padding. Reducing to four real support questions per page produced better extraction and a shorter, stronger page.

How to measure it

Mark up only questions that appear in your support queue. If a question has never been asked, its answer is unlikely to match what anyone queries.

Where it breaks

FAQ markup does not make a page authoritative. It improves extraction of content you already have, and cannot substitute for substance.

How it differs from adjacent terms

Answer Engine Optimization is the broader practice; FAQ schema is one concrete instrument for it. Compared with an AI Snippet, markup is a declaration that a question-answer pair exists, while the snippet is the text a system may lift. Marking up questions the page does not visibly answer produces neither.

Quick checklist

  • Every marked question visible and answered on the page.
  • Answers limited to two or three sentences.
  • Questions drawn from real support queries.
  • Visible wording and markup wording identical.
  • FAQ count kept to three to five per page.

FAQ

Does FAQ schema still produce rich results?

Rich result eligibility has narrowed, but the markup retains value for machine comprehension and for AI extraction, which is the more relevant benefit here.

How many FAQs per page?

Three to five is a practical range. Beyond that, the answers get thinner and the page starts to read as filler rather than substance.
