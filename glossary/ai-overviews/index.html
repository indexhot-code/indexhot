What is Google AI Overviews?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

Google AI Overviews is an AI-generated summary that appears above or within Google search results, compressing several sources into a short answer with attached links.

In short: The AI-generated summary block at the top of Google results.

Why it matters

It is the highest-volume AI answer surface in the world, and it sits directly on top of classic rankings. That makes it the clearest place where SEO eligibility and GEO extractability compound.

How to work with it

Two gates apply: the page must be eligible in Google's results, then it must be summarisable without losing accuracy. Long narrative sections rarely survive the second gate.

Common mistakes

  • Chasing the overview before the page ranks in the top ten.
  • Writing long introductions that push the answer below the fold of the summary.
  • Measuring impressions without tracking whether a link was attached.

Real example

A B2B site ranked third for a head term but was never included in the overview. The page opened with 300 words of context before the answer. Moving the definition into the first sentence and adding a comparison table lifted inclusion from zero to 40 percent of checks over one month.

How to measure it

Track overview inclusion separately from ranking position. A page can hold a top-five rank and still be excluded from the summary, and the fixes differ.

Where it breaks

Overview inclusion is unstable across queries and regions. It can appear for one phrasing and vanish for a near-identical one, so single-query checks overstate either success or failure.

How it differs from adjacent terms

An AI Snippet is the passage-level artefact; AI Overviews is a specific placement inside Google results with its own eligibility rules. Compared with Query Fan-Out, which is the retrieval technique behind the summary, AI Overviews is the visible output. The practical difference matters: fan-out rewards narrow pages that answer sub-questions, while overview inclusion still requires the page to be eligible in ordinary rankings first.

Quick checklist

  • Target page ranks in the top ten before chasing the overview.
  • Definition appears in the first sentence of the page.
  • Comparison or specification data published as text, not images.
  • Inclusion tracked separately from ranking position.
  • Re-checks repeated across several phrasings of the same query.

FAQ

Do I need to rank to appear in AI Overviews?

In most cases yes. The overview draws from pages already eligible in Google's results, so ranking remains the first gate before content shape decides selection.

Can AI Overviews mention my brand without linking me?

Yes. A summary can reference a brand or product without attaching a visible source link, which is why mention and citation must be measured separately.
