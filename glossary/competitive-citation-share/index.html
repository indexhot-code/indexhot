What is Competitive Citation Share?

Competitive citation share is the proportion of citations in a tracked prompt set that point to your domain, compared with the domains of the competitors you are measured against.

In short: Your share of cited sources versus competitors in a prompt set.

Why it matters

It converts citation counts into a market position. A brand can double its citations and still lose share if the category grows faster, and citation share exposes that immediately.

How to work with it

Fix the competitor domain list, count citations per domain across the same prompts, and report share rather than raw totals.

Common mistakes

  • Comparing raw citation counts across platforms with different source limits.
  • Changing the competitor set between reports.
  • Omitting third-party domains that mediate the category, such as review sites.

FAQ

How is citation share different from share of voice?

Share of voice counts brand mentions; citation share counts attributed sources. A brand can be mentioned often and cited rarely.

Should review sites be part of the competitor set?

They are worth tracking separately as intermediaries, because they often occupy the citation slot a brand wants.
