What is Citation?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

An AI citation is the attribution of a specific domain or page as a source inside a generated answer, usually shown as a link, a numbered reference or a named publication.

In short: Attribution of a domain as a source inside an AI answer.

Why it matters

Citations are the visible trace of grounding. They are how a reader verifies the answer and how a brand earns credibility inside a surface it does not control. They are also the most measurable part of the retrieval pipeline.

How to work with it

Track which URLs are cited, on which platform and for which prompt. Repeated citation of an unexpected page often reveals which content the model considers authoritative.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming only your homepage or blog can be cited.
  • Ignoring citations to pages you consider unimportant.
  • Treating citation count as equivalent across platforms.

Real example

A brand's most cited URL was not its homepage or a blog post but a support article explaining a compatibility limitation. That page answered a recurring question cleanly, so assistants treated it as the authoritative source for the whole category.

How to measure it

Audit which URLs are cited rather than which pages you consider important. The set is usually surprising and reveals what the engine treats as authoritative.

Where it breaks

A citation is not an endorsement. Many answers attach sources purely for verification while recommending a different product entirely.

How it differs from adjacent terms

Citation Rate is the frequency; a citation is one instance, and the URL behind it is the interesting part. Compared with Grounding, citation is the visible output while grounding is the mechanism that produced it. A citation to a page you consider unimportant is usually a signal about what the engine trusts, not a mistake to correct.

Quick checklist

  • Cited URL inventory built per engine per prompt cluster.
  • Support articles and documentation checked, not just marketing pages.
  • Third-party citations mapped to understand the wider source set.
  • Citations to outdated pages removed at the source.
  • No assumption that a citation implies endorsement.

FAQ

What is the difference between a mention and a citation?

A mention names the brand anywhere in the answer. A citation specifically attributes your domain as a source. A brand can be mentioned without being cited, and cited without being absorbed.

Can a citation be to a page I did not publish?

Yes. Models cite third-party pages about you, such as review sites, directories and press coverage, which is why off-site corroboration matters.
