What is Answer Sentiment?

Answer sentiment is the tone and framing an AI answer applies to a brand, ranging from recommendation and neutral description to caution, criticism or an explicit warning.

In short: The tone an AI answer uses when it discusses your brand.

Why it matters

Being present is not the same as being favoured. An answer that names you alongside a product recall, a pricing complaint or a privacy concern damages the brand more than silence would. Sentiment is the part of visibility that a mention rate cannot see.

How to work with it

Sample answers quarterly, code tone on a four-point scale, and separate factual criticism from tonal negativity, because they need different responses.

Common mistakes

  • Scoring sentiment with automated tools only, without reading the answers.
  • Ignoring hedged negatives such as 'popular, though some users report issues'.
  • Assuming a neutral tone is safe when the answer recommends a competitor instead.

FAQ

Can negative AI sentiment be fixed?

Sometimes. If it traces to a specific accurate criticism, the fix is usually operational. If it traces to outdated or third-party claims, publishing corrected, well-structured facts can gradually shift the sources the answer draws on.

How many answers should be coded for sentiment?

Enough to be stable for your category, typically the full prompt set for a quarterly read rather than a small sample.

Related terms
