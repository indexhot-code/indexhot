What is AI Search Visibility?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

AI search visibility is a measure of how often, how prominently and how deeply a brand appears in answers generated by AI assistants and AI-augmented search results. It is usually decomposed into mention, citation and absorption.

In short: How often, how prominently and how deeply a brand appears in AI answers.

Why it matters

A single composite score hides the difference between being named in passing and being the source an answer is built on. Splitting visibility into three levels lets teams see whether the problem is recognition, sourcing or substance.

How to work with it

Visibility is measured against a fixed question library, so the same prompts are asked on every platform and every cycle.

Common mistakes

  • Reporting one blended score with no breakdown by platform or metric.
  • Changing the prompt set between cycles and calling the difference progress.
  • Ignoring non-branded prompts, where new audiences actually discover brands.

Real example

Two competitors in the same category both scored 62 out of 100 on a blended index. Splitting the score showed one was mentioned in 44 prompts but cited in only 6, the other mentioned in 22 and cited in 18. The first had a recognition problem, the second a reach problem, and their fixes were opposite.

How to measure it

Always report the three components next to the composite, per platform. A blended number is useful for a dashboard, not for deciding what to do next.

Where it breaks

Visibility is not demand capture. High visibility in prompts your buyers never ask produces a good-looking report and no pipeline. Weight the library toward prompts tied to purchase intent.

How it differs from adjacent terms

Visibility is the umbrella term; Mention Rate, Citation Rate and Absorption Rate are its three components. Reporting visibility without the breakdown is like reporting revenue without a cost line: the number is real but useless for decisions. Compared with Share of Voice, visibility measures your absolute presence, while share measures your position against competitors. A brand can improve visibility every quarter and still lose share if the category grows faster than it does.

Quick checklist

  • Prompts frozen and versioned before the first measurement.
  • Branded and non-branded results reported separately.
  • Each platform reported as its own column, never averaged.
  • Competitor set fixed in advance and left unchanged between runs.
  • At least one absorption sample coded by hand each cycle.

FAQ

What counts as good AI search visibility?

There is no universal threshold. What matters is the trend against your own baseline and your position relative to the competitors named in the same answers.

Can visibility be bought?

Not directly. There is no paid placement inside generated answers. Visibility is earned through crawlable, well-structured, independently corroborated content.

Related terms

Mention Rate the share of tracked prompts in which your brand is named Citation Rate the share of prompts in which your domain is linked as a source Share of Voice your brand's share of mentions within a tracked prompt set Generative Engine Optimization the practice of making a brand visible, citable and quotable inside AI-generated answers Answer Engine Optimization optimising content to be chosen as the direct answer, not just a ranked result
