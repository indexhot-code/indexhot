What is Citation Rate (CR)?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

Citation Rate (CR) is the share of prompts in a fixed question library where your domain appears as an attributed source in the AI answer, typically as a link or a numbered reference.

In short: The share of prompts in which your domain is linked as a source.

Why it matters

Citation is stronger evidence than mention: it shows the system treated your page as usable evidence. It is also the metric that most directly reflects crawlability, extractability and grounding quality.

How to work with it

CR = prompts where your domain is cited / total prompts run. Track per platform, because citation density varies sharply between engines.

Common mistakes

  • Treating a citation as proof that your wording was used.
  • Ignoring citations to pages you did not intend to surface.
  • Comparing raw citation counts across platforms with different source limits.

Real example

Across an identical 50-prompt set, one brand held 61 percent citation rate on Perplexity and 14 percent on ChatGPT. The same pages were involved. Source-count differences, not content quality, explained most of the gap.

How to measure it

Never compare citation rate across engines without normalising for how many sources each attaches. Report the raw count and the rate together.

Where it breaks

Citation rate can rise while influence falls, if the new citations are to pages that say little. Track which URLs are being cited, not just how many.

How it differs from adjacent terms

A citation is the individual event; citation rate is its frequency across a fixed prompt set. Compared with Citation Absorption, rate asks whether your domain appeared, absorption asks whether your substance did. Perplexity can produce high rates and low absorption at the same time, which is why the two must never be combined into a single quality score.

Quick checklist

  • Cited URLs logged, not just citation totals.
  • Source-count differences normalised between engines.
  • Unexpected citations investigated as signals, not errors.
  • Third-party citations to your brand tracked separately.
  • Rate compared only against a fixed baseline set.

FAQ

Why is my citation rate higher on Perplexity than ChatGPT?

Perplexity attaches far more sources per answer, so citation opportunities are more numerous. The same page can be cited there while contributing little to the wording.

Does a citation always send traffic?

No. Citations are often weakly surfaced, and many users never click them. Citation measures evidence use, not visits.
