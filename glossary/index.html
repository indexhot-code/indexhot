The IndexHot GEO Glossary

50 terms used in AI search visibility work, each defined in one sentence and explained in context. Written for teams who need to measure and improve brand presence in generated answers.

Core concepts

What GEO is and the surfaces it targets.

Generative Engine Optimization the practice of making a brand visible, citable and quotable inside AI-generated answers Answer Engine Optimization optimising content to be chosen as the direct answer, not just a ranked result AI Search Visibility how often, how prominently and how deeply a brand appears in AI answers Answer Engine a search system that returns a direct answer instead of a list of links Generative Search search that synthesises a new answer from retrieved sources Zero-Click Search queries resolved on the results or answer surface without a visit Google AI Overviews the AI-generated summary block at the top of Google results AI Snippet a short machine-generated passage lifted from sources to answer a query

Retrieval and models

How systems find and ground on your content.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation retrieving external documents before generating an answer Grounding anchoring generated output to verifiable external sources Hallucination confidently stated output with no basis in the sources Query Fan-Out splitting one query into several sub-queries searched in parallel Semantic Search retrieval by meaning rather than exact keyword match Vector Embeddings numeric representations of text used for similarity matching Chunking splitting documents into passages for retrieval and embedding Re-ranking a second pass that re-scores retrieved passages for relevance Hybrid Search combining keyword matching and vector similarity in one retrieval pass Context Window the maximum amount of text a model can consider at once

Measurement

The metrics that make visibility reproducible.

Mention Rate the share of tracked prompts in which your brand is named Citation Rate the share of prompts in which your domain is linked as a source Absorption Rate the share of prompts where your specific claims enter the answer Citation Absorption how deeply a cited source's content is reused in the answer Share of Voice your brand's share of mentions within a tracked prompt set Citation attribution of a domain as a source inside an AI answer Branded Prompt a question that includes the brand name explicitly Non-Branded Prompt a category question asked without naming any brand Governed Question Library a fixed, versioned set of prompts used for every measurement run AI Visibility Index a ranked index of brands scored on the same AI prompt set Citation Gap the difference between being mentioned and being cited as a source Answer Sentiment the tone an AI answer uses when it discusses your brand Long-Tail Prompt a specific, constrained question with clear intent and low volume Citation Source Diversity how spread out the sources behind an AI answer are

Entity and content

How machines identify and interpret your brand.

Entity Clarity how unambiguously a model can identify your brand as a distinct entity Knowledge Graph a structured network of entities and their relationships Schema Markup structured data vocabulary embedded in pages to describe entities JSON-LD a JSON format for embedding structured data in web pages llms.txt a proposed file that summarises a site for language models Topical Authority depth and consistency of coverage across a topic Brand Entity your brand as a machine-resolvable object with attributes and relations Organization Schema structured markup that declares your organisation as an entity FAQ Schema markup that pairs questions with answers for machine extraction

Technical foundations

Crawl, index and extractability prerequisites.

Content Extractability how easily a machine can isolate, quote and reuse your content Crawlability whether crawlers can discover and fetch your pages Indexability whether a page is eligible to be stored in a search index Canonical Tag a signal declaring the preferred URL for a piece of content Internal Linking links between your own pages that distribute authority and signal structure AI Referral Traffic visits that arrive from an AI answer or assistant Competitive Citation Share your share of cited sources versus competitors in a prompt set AI Crawler a bot that fetches content for model training or live retrieval Content Freshness how current a page is, and how clearly it says so
