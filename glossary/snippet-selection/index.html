What is Snippet Selection?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

Snippet selection is the moment a retrieved passage becomes part of an answer. Between finding a page and quoting it, a model chooses specific sentences, and that choice is governed by how self-contained and verifiable those sentences are.

In short: The step where a model chooses which sentences of retrieved material to carry into the answer.

The gap between retrieval and quotation

Retrieval produces a set of documents or chunks. Generation produces an answer, and the answer is assembled from sentences the model chooses to carry. A page can be retrieved and then contribute nothing, which shows up in reporting as a citation with no absorption.

Snippet selection explains that pattern. The model did read the page. It found nothing it could lift without inventing connective tissue, so it paraphrased around it and cited the page as context rather than evidence.

Which sentences get chosen

  • Sentences whose meaning survives removal from the surrounding text.
  • Sentences carrying a checkable fact: a number, a date, a named entity, a stated condition.
  • Sentences short enough to be reproduced without distortion, but specific enough to be worth reproducing.
  • Sentences whose claims the model can corroborate elsewhere, since an unsupported specific is a risk to repeat.

What this means for a section's opening

The first two sentences of a section are the most likely candidates, because they are where a claim is usually stated most cleanly. If those sentences are throat-clearing, the model moves on and takes the middle of the section instead, or takes nothing.

The practical rule: write the opening sentence as though it will be quoted alone in a different document, because it probably will be.

Diagnosing selection failures

When a page is cited but the answer says nothing of yours, the failure is usually selection rather than retrieval. Read the answer next to the page and ask which sentences could have been lifted unchanged. If the answer is none, that is the finding.

Frequently asked questions

Is snippet selection under my control?

Indirectly. You control the sentences available to it, which is most of the outcome.

Does markup influence it?

Marginally. Structure helps a model locate candidates; it does not make a weak sentence strong.

Why was a middle paragraph used and not my summary?

Because that paragraph was more self-contained or more verifiable. Move the specific claim into the opening sentence if you want it preferred.
