What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of formatting content so that answer engines, including AI assistants, voice assistants and featured-snippet systems, select it as the direct response to a user's question.

In short: Optimising content to be chosen as the direct answer, not just a ranked result.

Why it matters

AEO predates generative AI: featured snippets and voice answers already rewarded pages that answered a question in one clean passage. Generative engines extended that logic to entire conversations, which made answer-shaped content a requirement rather than a bonus.

How to work with it

AEO usually means leading with a complete answer, keeping sentences self-contained, and marking up FAQs so the machine can pair question and answer unambiguously.

Common mistakes

  • Burying the answer below three paragraphs of brand narrative.
  • Writing answers that depend on surrounding context to make sense.
  • Marking up FAQ content that is not actually visible on the page.

Real example

A recipe site rewrote each of its 60 dish pages to open with a two-sentence answer naming time, servings and difficulty. Featured-snippet capture went from 12 pages to 38, and the same opening sentences became the passages most often reused in AI answers. The rewrite cost under a day of editing.

How to measure it

Copy the first 40 words of each page into a blank document. If the excerpt does not answer a complete question on its own, the page fails the extractability test regardless of its length.

Where it breaks

Answer-first formatting hurts narrative content. For opinion, brand story and investigative pieces, compressing the opening into a definition strips the reason readers came. Apply AEO where the intent is informational.

How it differs from adjacent terms

GEO and AEO are frequently used interchangeably, but the mechanisms differ. AEO optimises for systems that lift a passage verbatim, so exact wording and placement matter. GEO optimises for systems that rewrite, so claim structure and verifiability matter more than phrasing. Compared with an AI Snippet, which is the artefact produced, AEO is the practice that makes the artefact possible. A page can be AEO-perfect and still fail GEO if its entity signals are ambiguous or its facts are unsourced.

Quick checklist

  • The first two sentences answer the question without context.
  • Each answer stands alone when copied out of the page.
  • FAQ markup covers only questions visible on the page.
  • No claim depends on the paragraph above it.
  • A page exists for each sub-question, not one page for all.

FAQ

How is AEO different from GEO?

AEO is the broader discipline of winning any direct-answer surface, including voice and featured snippets. GEO specifically targets generative engines that synthesise multiple sources into new text.

Does AEO still matter in 2026?

Yes. Answer-shaped content is the raw material every generative engine extracts, so AEO practices remain the foundation of GEO work.
