What is Absorption Rate (AR)?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

Absorption Rate (AR) is the share of prompts in a fixed question library where your brand's specific facts, framing or recommendations are carried into the answer text, not merely referenced.

In short: The share of prompts where your specific claims enter the answer.

Why it matters

Absorption is the metric closest to influence. An answer that repeats your differentiator persuades on your behalf; an answer that lists your domain without using your content does not.

How to work with it

AR requires coding each answer against your source material, checking whether distinctive claims, numbers or recommendations appear. It is the most labour-intensive metric and the most decision-relevant.

Common mistakes

  • Approximating absorption with keyword overlap in the answer.
  • Scoring absorption on generic claims any competitor could supply.
  • Reporting AR without auditing a sample of coded answers.

Real example

A cybersecurity vendor was cited in 30 percent of prompts but absorbed in 5. Its pages described capabilities generically; a competitor stated specific thresholds and time-to-detect figures. The specific page won the answer text despite fewer citations.

How to measure it

Code a sample of answers against a short list of distinctive claims from your page. If none of your claims can be identified as yours, absorption is effectively zero.

Where it breaks

Absorption scoring is judgement, not arithmetic. Without a written rubric and a second coder, the number drifts toward whatever the analyst expects to see.

How it differs from adjacent terms

Citation Absorption names the phenomenon; absorption rate measures how often it happens. Compared with Mention Rate, absorption is a different order of evidence: it means the answer adopted your framing rather than merely recognising your name. A brand with modest mentions and strong absorption usually out-influences a brand with the reverse profile.

Quick checklist

  • A short list of distinctive claims written down before scoring.
  • Two coders scoring the same sample independently.
  • Generic claims excluded, since any competitor could supply them.
  • Absorption coded per answer, not inferred from keywords.
  • Disagreements between coders resolved by rubric, not discussion.

FAQ

How is absorption measured objectively?

By coding each answer against a fixed list of distinctive claims from your page. Two coders and a written rubric keep the judgement reproducible.

Why is absorption low on Perplexity?

Perplexity synthesises across many sources and paraphrases heavily. High citation volume coexists with shallow reuse, so citation alone understates the gap.
