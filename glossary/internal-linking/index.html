What is Internal Linking?

Last updated November 2026 · Reviewed against the IndexHot GEO Measurement Standard v1.0

Internal linking is the practice of connecting pages within your own site with descriptive anchor text, guiding crawlers, distributing authority and making topical relationships explicit.

In short: Links between your own pages that distribute authority and signal structure.

Why it matters

New pages are discovered and prioritised largely through internal links. A strong page with no internal links is a page crawlers visit rarely, and a cluster with no internal structure is a cluster only in your planning document.

How to work with it

Link hub to spokes and spokes back to the hub, use descriptive anchors, and keep every important page within a few clicks of the homepage.

Common mistakes

  • Orphaning new pages that are reachable only from the sitemap.
  • Using 'click here' anchors that convey no topic signal.
  • Linking every page to every other page, which flattens the hierarchy.

Real example

Twenty-one glossary pages were reachable only through sitemap and two related-term links. After adding category links from platform and industry pages, crawl frequency on those URLs rose and new pages began appearing in indexes within days instead of weeks.

How to measure it

Count inbound internal links per page. Anything under three is effectively half-orphaned, regardless of how good the content is.

Where it breaks

Internal links distribute attention, not authority evenly. A hundred links from one low-value page do less than five from a page that is itself frequently crawled.

How it differs from adjacent terms

Topical Authority is the impression a cluster creates; internal linking is how it is created and made legible. Compared with Crawlability, links determine both access and priority, so a page with no inbound links is crawlable in theory and nearly invisible in practice.

Quick checklist

  • Inbound internal links counted per page; under three is a defect.
  • Descriptive anchors naming the topic, not 'click here'.
  • Every new page linked from a relevant existing page on publish.
  • Hub and spoke links running in both directions.
  • Important pages within three clicks of the homepage.

FAQ

Do internal links affect AI visibility?

Yes, indirectly but materially. Internal links drive discovery and help establish which pages belong to the same topic, both of which influence retrieval.

How many internal links should a page have?

Enough to place it clearly in the site structure. Contextual links to related pages matter more than a fixed count.
