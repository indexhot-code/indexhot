What is AI Snippet?

An AI snippet is a short passage generated or extracted by an AI system to answer a query directly, usually drawn from one or more source documents and shown in place of a conventional result.

In short: A short machine-generated passage lifted from sources to answer a query.

Why it matters

Snippets are the atomic unit of AI visibility. If your claim cannot survive being compressed into two sentences, it will not appear, regardless of how strong the underlying page is.

How to work with it

Snippet-friendly content states the conclusion first, keeps each sentence self-contained and attaches a number or date to the claim.

Common mistakes

  • Using pronouns whose referent disappears when the sentence is lifted.
  • Hiding the key figure in an image or a collapsed table.
  • Writing one long paragraph with several competing claims.

FAQ

How is an AI snippet different from a featured snippet?

A featured snippet is extracted verbatim from a page. An AI snippet may be rewritten or synthesised from multiple sources, so wording can change while meaning is preserved.

Can I control what gets snipped?

Indirectly. Clear, self-contained sentences near the top of a section are far more likely to be selected than dense prose where the claim is spread across paragraphs.
